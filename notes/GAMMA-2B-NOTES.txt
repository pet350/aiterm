AITerm 0.9.14-gamma-2b
Based on the gamma-2a source archive.

- Added HostDetails.source_flags (bit flags) independent of the legacy primary source.
- Local, Systemd and SNMP collectors mark their own source membership.
- SNMP import clears stale SNMP membership and removes SNMP-only host records before rebuilding observations; mixed-source records survive.
- Local refresh preserves other collectors' source flags and the newer last-update timestamp.
- /hosts dump and host formatter report all collector sources.
- /hosts status continues to count local, SNMP-only and other unique host records.
- SNMP poller unchanged; SNMP observations still snapshotted under the SNMP lock before Host Analysis lock.
